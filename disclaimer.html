This website is for informational and appointment-enquiry purposes only.


Not emergency support

Online counselling and mental wellness support provided through this website are not substitutes for emergency mental health services, medical diagnosis, psychiatric treatment, or urgent crisis care.


If you are in crisis

If you are experiencing thoughts of self-harm, harm to others, severe distress, or any emergency, please contact local emergency services immediately. You may also contact crisis support helplines such as iCall: 9152987821 or Vandrevala Foundation: 1860-2662-345.


Results vary

Counselling progress varies from person to person. No website content, session description, or resource should be understood as a guaranteed outcome.


Professional scope

If your concern requires psychiatric, medical, or emergency intervention, you may be advised to contact an appropriate healthcare provider or emergency service.
