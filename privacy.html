Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. Information shared through WhatsApp or the booking form is used only to respond to your enquiry and schedule online counselling or wellness sessions.


Information collected

Name, phone number, preferred timing, session type, and the concern you choose to share.


How it is used

Only for appointment coordination, counselling communication, and follow-up related to your enquiry.


Confidentiality

Online counselling is handled with care and confidentiality. However, this website and WhatsApp are not emergency-support channels.


Emergency

If you are in crisis or at risk of harm, contact local emergency services or crisis helplines immediately.
