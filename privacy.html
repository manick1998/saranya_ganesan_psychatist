Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how information shared through this website, WhatsApp, or appointment enquiry forms is used.


Information collected

When you request an appointment, you may share your name, phone number, email address, preferred timing, session type, and a brief description of your concern.


How information is used

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry, coordinate appointment scheduling, share session information, and provide follow-up related to your request.


Confidentiality

Information shared for counselling is handled with care and confidentiality within professional and legal limits. Confidentiality may have exceptions where there is serious or imminent risk to your safety or the safety of another person.


WhatsApp and third-party platforms

WhatsApp or online meeting platforms may be used for communication and scheduling. These services have their own privacy practices, so please avoid sharing highly sensitive emergency information through website forms.


Data retention and deletion

Appointment enquiry details are retained only as long as needed for communication and session coordination. You may request deletion of your enquiry details by contacting through WhatsApp.


Emergency note

This website is not an emergency support channel. If you are in crisis or at risk of harm, contact local emergency services or crisis helplines immediately.
